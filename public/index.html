How do I get the goods?

When products launch, product pages will link to Etsy, Gumroad, or both for checkout and digital delivery. Choose your preferred store when both are available. The current catalog contains design samples, so checkout is not available yet.

What software do I need?

Each design will be available for Canva, Adobe Express, and Figma. Choose the version that works for you and customize it in your preferred platform.

Can I use these for client work?

Yes, choose the Commercial Use option for business or client projects. Personal Use is for your own personal projects. Each template will have separate purchase options, with full license terms provided on its Etsy or Gumroad listing. Review those terms before purchasing.

Are these custom designs?

Yes. Every Schmutz template is an original design, ready for you to make your own. Customize the text, colors, images, and layout to fit your project. Client-specific customization is not included or offered.
