The catalog studio.

Add products, upload cover artwork, and attach Etsy and Gumroad listings. This editor saves a draft in this browser. Export your catalog and replace public/catalog.json in GitHub to publish the changes.

Uploads here are cover images only. Deliver the actual template files through your Etsy or Gumroad listing. This is a local editing tool, not a hosted account or upload service.
