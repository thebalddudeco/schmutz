The catalog studio.

Add products, upload cover artwork, and attach Etsy listings. This editor saves a draft in this browser. Export your catalog and replace public/catalog.json in GitHub to publish the changes.

Uploads here are cover images only. Deliver the actual template files through your Etsy listing. This is a local editing tool, not a hosted account or upload service.

Turning off “sample” requires a valid Etsy listing link. Drafts stay on this device until exported and committed to GitHub.
