## Main description

Studying for the GED can feel overwhelming when everything is scattered across random worksheets, videos, and practice links. GED Prep Hub brings the work into one place.

This is a browser-based GED study app built for adult learners who want structure, repetition, progress tracking, and a calmer way to prepare. It includes diagnostics, subject-by-subject practice, answer explanations, essay work, progress dashboards, timed practice exams, bookmarks, wrong-answer review, and study plans.

It is designed to feel more like a real study system and less like a pile of disconnected quizzes.

### What is included

- Dashboard with study progress, readiness signals, recent activity, and next-step guidance
- Diagnostic Center to identify weak areas before building a study plan
- Math practice bank with skill filters and explanations
- Reasoning Through Language Arts practice with reading, editing, and passage-based work
- Science practice with experiment, graph, and evidence questions
- Social Studies practice with civics, economics, history, and source analysis
- Essay Lab with prompts, draft space, rubric support, and self-review tools
- Timed practice exam modes
- Progress Tracker with bookmarks, wrong-answer review, and subject analytics
- Study Plan section for structured pacing
- Local browser save state through `localStorage`
- Gumroad email + order-ID gate for hosted buyer access

### Who this is for

- Adult learners preparing for the GED
- Tutors or parents helping an adult learner study
- Buyers who want a focused self-study tool instead of a generic worksheet pack
- People who want to study from a browser without installing software

### How access works

After purchase, you will receive the hosted app link and a Gumroad receipt that includes your order ID. Enter the same email used at checkout plus the order ID to unlock the app in your browser.

The app remembers verified access on that device unless browser storage is cleared.

### Technical requirements

- A current browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari
- Internet access to open the hosted app
- Browser storage enabled for saved progress on that device

### Important notes

- This is a self-study product, not live tutoring
- Progress saves locally in the browser used to study
- If you switch devices or clear browser storage, you may need to enter your order ID again
- Access is intended for the purchaser only

## FAQ

### Is this a downloadable PDF?

No. This is a browser-based study app.

### Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the hosted link in your browser and unlock it with your purchase email and Gumroad order ID.

### Will my progress save?

Yes. Progress is saved in your browser on that device.

### Can I use it on more than one device?

You can unlock it on another device, but saved progress is local to each browser unless you manually keep them in sync.

### Is this official GED testing software?

No. It is an independent study tool designed to help learners prepare more consistently and confidently.

### Is this good for adult learners?

Yes. The structure, tone, and study flow were designed for adult learners who want a serious prep experience without childish presentation.
