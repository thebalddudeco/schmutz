Project goal:
Create a full GED prep platform that can realistically help an adult learner prepare for the GED and improve readiness for college-level work.

Tech constraints:
- HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript only
- No backend
- No frameworks
- Use localStorage for persistence
- Organize code into maintainable files and folders
- Everything must run locally in-browser

Required deliverables:
- index.html
- styles.css
- app.js
- data/questions.js
- data/passages.js
- data/essays.js
- README.md

You may add more files if it improves structure.

Core product requirements:
Build these sections:
1. Dashboard
2. Diagnostic Center
3. Math
4. Reasoning Through Language Arts
5. Science
6. Social Studies
7. Essay Lab
8. Practice Exams
9. Progress Tracker
10. Study Plan
11. Montana GED Info

Question bank requirements:
- Math: at least 100 questions
- RLA: at least 100 questions
- Science: at least 100 questions
- Social Studies: at least 100 questions
- Mixed / full-bank practice: at least 100 questions
- Total target: 500 to 700 questions

Question variety:
Use a real mix of:
- multiple choice
- passage-based questions
- revision/editing questions
- data interpretation
- multi-step reasoning
- critical thinking questions
- constructed-response prompts where appropriate
- GED-style essay prompts in Essay Lab

Do not make the questions feel lazy, repetitive, or obviously auto-generated. Vary context, difficulty, and skill type. Prefer adult-relevant contexts such as work, schedules, construction, money, transportation, applications, civic life, health, job training, and college readiness.

Difficulty model:
Every subject must support:
- Foundation
- GED Standard
- College Readiness Stretch

The learner must be able to filter or move through these levels.

UI and UX requirements:
The app should feel like a serious paid prep platform for adults.
Design goals:
- dark, polished, adult-friendly

- clear navigation

Must-have UX features:

- retake wrong answers mode
- resume where you left off

- recommended next study action

Behavior examples:

- if inference accuracy is low, recommend passage practice
- if essay completion is low, recommend Essay Lab

Content quality standards:

- avoid filler
