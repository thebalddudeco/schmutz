A few things
worth knowing.

How do I get the goods?

Choose Buy on Etsy or Buy on Gumroad. Etsy purchases include an access PDF with your Canva link. Gumroad purchases provide the Canva link and editing instructions directly in your purchase content. Open the link to create your editable copy. This is a digital product; nothing is shipped.

What software do I need?

You need a Canva account and an internet connection. Edit the text, photos and colors in Canva. Check the product listing for Canva Pro requirements; Pro elements may need a Pro plan or replacement with free content before export.

How can I use the templates?

Use the templates for your own personal or business social-media content under the terms on the product listing. Do not resell, share or redistribute the editable template or access link as a competing template product. Review the listing before purchasing.

Can I customize the designs?

Yes. Each template is ready for you to make your own. Customize the text, colors, images, and layout to fit your project. Client-specific customization is not included or offered.
